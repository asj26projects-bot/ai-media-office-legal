Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This application ("AI Media Office / The Unsolved File") is an internal content publishing and analytics tool used to manage our own official social media channels on YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok.

1. Data We Access

When authorized via OAuth, the application accesses only the permissions explicitly granted by the channel owner (such as video publishing, basic profile info, post analytics, and comment/message management).

2. How Data Is Used

Data is used solely to publish original short-form videos, set synthetic media disclosures, evaluate content performance, and respond to community comments or messages. We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties.

3. Data Storage & Deletion

OAuth tokens and analytics metrics are stored locally and securely on the operator's machine. Access can be revoked at any time from your TikTok, Google, or Meta account security settings.

4. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the application administrator via the official channel profile.
